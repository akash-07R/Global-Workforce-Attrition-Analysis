# Global Workforce & Attrition Intelligence

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze workforce composition,
employee attrition, compensation, performance, and employee experience.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how Power BI, Power Query, and DAX can be used
to transform an HR dataset into an interactive workforce analytics solution.

The dashboard is presented through a fictional MNC workforce analytics
scenario and focuses on identifying workforce patterns that can support
data-driven HR analysis.

## Business Objectives

- Understand overall workforce composition
- Monitor employee attrition and attrition rate
- Identify workforce segments with different attrition patterns
- Analyze compensation across job roles and job levels
- Examine performance distribution
- Explore employee satisfaction and workplace experience
- Provide an interactive management-oriented dashboard

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of:

- Total employees
- Employee attrition
- Attrition rate
- Average monthly income
- Workforce by department
- Attrition by job role
- Attrition across age groups
- Attrition by company tenure

### 2. Attrition Analysis

Explores attrition patterns across:

- Overtime
- Business travel
- Salary bands
- Job roles
- Gender
- Job satisfaction

### 3. Compensation & Performance

Analyzes:

- Average monthly income by job role
- Income by job level
- Income by tenure
- Performance rating distribution
- Performance by job level
- Salary hike by job level

### 4. Employee Experience

Examines:

- Job satisfaction
- Work-life balance
- Environment satisfaction
- Job involvement
- Relationship satisfaction

## Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query.

Key steps included:

- Data quality validation
- Duplicate checking
- Removal of redundant fields
- Data type validation
- Creation of age groups
- Creation of salary bands
- Creation of company tenure bands
- Validation of categorical fields

## DAX Measures

Key measures include:

- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Job Satisfaction
- Average Work-Life Balance
- Average Environment Satisfaction

## Dataset

The project uses the public IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition &
Performance dataset for educational and portfolio purposes by Kaggle.

The dataset is not proprietary company data.
